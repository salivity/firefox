Accessing Third-Party AI Models in the Firefox Sidebar

This article provides a complete overview of how the Mozilla Firefox web browser integrates third-party artificial intelligence language models directly into its interface. By utilizing a built-in sidebar feature, Firefox allows users to seamlessly interact with popular AI platforms alongside their main browsing window without needing to switch tabs or install external extensions. Below, we cover the mechanics of how this integration functions, the contextual tools it offers, the supported AI providers, and how to enable the feature.

The Mechanics of the Sidebar Integration

Firefox provides access to third-party Large Language Models (LLMs) by embedding a dedicated, persistent web panel within the browser's native sidebar. Instead of forcing users to rely on a single, proprietary AI built into the browser, Mozilla acts as a bridge to existing web-based AI services.

When you select an AI provider, Firefox loads a compact version of that specific service's web interface directly next to your active webpage. You authenticate your session by logging into your existing account for that AI service. Because it runs in a segregated sidebar panel, you can navigate to different websites in your main window while keeping your AI conversation open and active on the side.

Contextual Webpage Interaction

The integration goes beyond simply hosting a chat window; it also connects the content of your active webpage to the AI model. When reading an article or document, you can highlight any piece of text and right-click to bring up the browser context menu.

Firefox injects specific AI actions into this menu. With a single click, you can instruct the sidebar AI to:

  • Summarize the highlighted text.
  • Explain complex concepts in simpler terms.
  • Quiz you on the selected material to test your comprehension.

When you select one of these options, Firefox automatically copies the highlighted text, pairs it with a pre-written system prompt, and submits it directly to the third-party model currently active in your sidebar.

Supported Third-Party Models

Because the integration relies on standard web technologies, Firefox makes it easy to switch between different AI ecosystems on the fly. Users are not locked into one model and can choose from several leading providers, including:

  • Anthropic Claude
  • ChatGPT (OpenAI)
  • Google Gemini
  • HuggingChat
  • Le Chat Mistral

You can switch your active model at any time using a dropdown menu at the top of the sidebar.

How to Enable the AI Chatbot Sidebar

The AI integration is strictly opt-in and can be activated through Firefox's experimental settings. To set it up:

  1. Open Firefox and click the menu button in the top-right corner, then select Settings.
  2. Select Firefox Labs from the left-hand navigation menu.
  3. Locate the AI Chatbot option and check the box to enable it.
  4. Choose your preferred AI provider from the radio buttons that appear.

Once enabled, the sidebar will automatically open, or you can toggle it at any time by clicking the sidebar icon in your browser toolbar.

Privacy and Data Handling

Because this feature uses third-party models, Firefox acts only as a conduit. The browser does not automatically feed your browsing history or webpage data to the AI. Information is only shared with the third-party provider when you actively open the sidebar, log in, and submit a prompt or use the right-click context menu to send highlighted text. Any data you submit is subject to the specific privacy policy and terms of service of the AI provider you have chosen to use.
